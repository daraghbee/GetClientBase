Questions

What do I get for free?

Everything. The free version has every feature and is limited only by size: 15 clients and 15 jobs. ClientBase Pro removes both limits.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. ClientBase runs on iPhone and iPad, and one subscription covers both.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. There is no ClientBase account and we do not hold a copy of your client list. Read the privacy policy for the detail.

What happens if I lose or replace my phone?

Choose a backup folder in iCloud Drive and ClientBase copies each automatic backup there. On the new phone, open the backup file and your clients, jobs, invoices and receipts are restored.

Do I need a signal to use it?

No. Your clients, bookings and money are all available offline. Maps and weather need a connection.

How do I cancel?

In the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and your data stays in the app.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export your client list as CSV, your ledger as PDF or CSV, or a full backup file at any time.
